ACTIVE STUDENT INTERNSHIP

Community Connect Internship

Make a difference & gain real experience! Open to all undergraduate, postgraduate, and diploma students in Hyderabad passionate about community service.

Call: +91 91862 11983

1. School Renovation & Infrastructure

Help paint classrooms, repair facilities, set up child-friendly spaces, and create welcoming learning environments.

2. Stationery & Learning Materials

Organize and distribute notebooks, bags, stationery kits, and school uniforms to underprivileged children.

3. Student Skill Development & Digital Library

Mentor students in digital literacy, communication, computer fundamentals, career awareness, and practical life skills.

4. Women Empowerment & Training

Support vocational training, financial literacy sessions, entrepreneurship guidance, and women self-help initiatives.
