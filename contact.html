Frequently Asked Questions

What are the minimum hours required to volunteer?
There is no rigid requirement! Most of our volunteers dedicate 2 to 4 hours on Saturday or Sunday mornings. You can participate on weekends that match your schedule.
How do I receive an 80G tax exemption certificate for my donation?
After completing your PhonePe, UPI or bank donation, submit your PAN number and transaction reference on our Donate page or email founderwvfindia@wvfindia.org. We issue an official 80G receipt via email within 48 hours.
Can our company partner with White Volunteers Foundation for CSR projects?
Yes! We are an MCA-registered CSR-1 implementing agency eligible under Schedule VII for Education, Healthcare, Sanitation, and Rural Development projects. Contact our CSR team at founderwvfindia@wvfindia.org for customized proposals.
How do I apply for the Community Connect Internship in Hyderabad?
Any undergraduate, postgraduate, or diploma student in Hyderabad can apply via our Volunteer page or call/WhatsApp +91 91862 11983. You will receive verified certificates and hands-on community fieldwork experience.
