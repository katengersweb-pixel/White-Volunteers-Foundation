Nityapalamrutam: Eradicating Chronic Malnutrition

Way back in 2012, Shekhar Maraveni, an officer of the Indian Armed Forces, observed the sufferings of tribal and poor people who couldn't afford proper clothing and food.

In Usoor, a remote village in Bijapur District, Chhattisgarh, he observed malnourished children with minimal clothing. Many children had never seen a school in their life. Even if they went to school, dropout ratios were high due to morning hunger and lack of uniforms.

He resolved that no child in India should drop out of school for these reasons, giving birth to White Volunteers Foundation.

The Nityapalamrutam Mission:

Delivering 150 ml of pure milk to every kid attending school to combat chronic malnutrition. Under this program, 5 government schools have already been adopted in Telangana State.

Vastradaan (Clothing Drives)

Clothing is a basic need for humans. We aim to donate clothes to those who are in need. We took the initiative and distributed clothing across many slums and underserved neighborhoods of Greater Hyderabad.

Active in urban slums & rural hamlets

Bhuddhishakti (Scholarships)

Research shows that many talented underprivileged children drop out of school due to the financial challenges of their families. To support these talented students and encourage their education, WVF India provides dedicated scholarships.

Merit & Need-based financial grants

#BRAINYBIRDS Initiative

A unique social service concept for schools and children running every year from June 1st to May 31st.

Why Community Volunteering for School Children:

  • 3. Strengthens social responsibility
  • 4. Practical real-world approach
  • 5. Motivates academic efforts and purpose

What Participating Schools Offer:

  • 2. Mandatory structured volunteer hours
  • 3. Reflection sessions and leadership development
