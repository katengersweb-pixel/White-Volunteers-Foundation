About Us

Empowering Children. Strengthening Schools. Transforming Communities.

Creating Sustainable Impact for Children, Youth & Women

White Volunteers Foundation is a social-impact organization committed to creating better opportunities for children, students, women, and underserved communities.

We work primarily with Government Schools and communities to improve educational infrastructure, provide essential learning resources, promote digital education, develop practical skills, and empower women to build stronger and more independent futures.

Our approach is simple: identify real needs, take practical action, and create sustainable impact.

The Genesis & Nityapalamrutam:

In 2012, Shekhar Maraveni, a dedicated changemaker and officer of the Indian Armed Forces, witnessed the extreme hardship of tribal children in Usoor (Bijapur Dist., Chhattisgarh). Malnutrition and lack of clothing caused alarming school dropouts. Determined that no child in India should leave school due to hunger or basic deprivation, he spearheaded grassroots educational seva that led to the founding of White Volunteers Foundation.

This gave birth to Nityapalamrutam, delivering 150 ml of pure milk daily to every school-going child to eliminate chronic malnutrition, now recognized across national media.
